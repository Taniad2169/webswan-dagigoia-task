Your surgery, start to finish

Clear steps before and after your procedure

  1. Consultation. Bring your insurance card, photo ID, medication list and your actual X-ray, MRI or scan films or CDs.
  2. Pre-surgery. Nothing to eat or drink for 8 hours. Arrange a ride, and we can set up rehab or home health before a joint replacement.
  3. Recovery. You go home with written instructions and prescriptions. Our answering service is available at any hour.
