Your First Visit at Conger Chiropractic

We only accept patients we believe we can help, so your first visit is all about understanding you.

1. Free consultation

A no-charge, no-obligation consultation to talk about your pain, your health history and your goals.

2. Exam (and x-rays if needed)

A chiropractic exam, plus x-rays only when they will change how we treat you.

3. Your care plan

We explain what we found, how chiropractic care may help and what your plan looks like. Your first two visits take about 45 minutes; follow-ups usually take 10–20 minutes.

Insurance

We accept most major insurance plans. Call (801) 530-4802 and we will check your coverage before your visit.
