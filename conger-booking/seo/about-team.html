Meet Dr. Ted L. Conger and Our Team

Conger Chiropractic is a full-service chiropractic clinic in downtown Salt Lake City, Utah. Our team combines decades of chiropractic experience with massage therapy and friendly, personal service.

Dr. Ted L. Conger, DC

Chiropractor · Founder

Dr. Conger completed his pre-chiropractic studies at Brigham Young University and earned his Doctor of Chiropractic degree at Palmer College of Chiropractic in Davenport, Iowa, the founding college of the chiropractic profession.

  • Two-time president of the Utah Chiropractic Physicians Association (1988–89 and 1997–99), and a board member in several other roles
  • More than 500 hours of continuing education since 1983, including clinical x-ray, adjusting technique, spinal trauma, sports injuries, pediatrics, nutrition, rehabilitation, ethics and HIPAA compliance

Born and raised in Seattle, Washington, Dr. Conger is an Eagle Scout. He and his wife, Debbie, have two daughters and five grandchildren.

Luke Jensen, LMT

Licensed Massage Therapist

Luke graduated from UCMT (Utah College of Massage Therapy) in 2017 and completed its Master program. He specializes in trigger point, deep tissue and sports massage.

Before massage therapy, Luke served four years in the U.S. Army as a health care specialist and combat medic. He is a Utah native and an Eagle Scout.

Tiffany

Office Manager & Executive Assistant

Tiffany has been part of Conger Chiropractic since 2000 and full-time since 2008, working in reception, as a chiropractic assistant, in marketing and on special events. She holds a bachelor's degree from Brigham Young University and grew up in Salt Lake City.

Pilar
