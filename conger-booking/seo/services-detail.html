Chiropractic Care for Pain Relief in Salt Lake City

Chiropractic has helped people since 1895. At Conger Chiropractic, our goal is to relieve pain and restore movement naturally, so you can avoid surgery and long-term pain medication whenever possible. Every new patient starts with a no-charge, no-obligation consultation and an exam.

Lower & Upper Back Pain

Back pain from lifting, sitting, sports or a strained muscle is the most common reason patients see us. Adjustments, spinal decompression and targeted exercises help restore movement and ease pain.

Neck & Shoulder Pain

Stiff necks and sore shoulders often come from posture, desk work, sleep position or injury. Gentle adjustments and soft-tissue work help you turn your head and move your arms comfortably again.

Headaches

Many headaches start with tension in the neck and upper back. Care focuses on the joints and muscles that feed those headaches, plus posture and ergonomic advice.

Sciatica, Numbness & Tingling

Pain, numbness or tingling that travels down the leg or arm can mean an irritated nerve. We look for the source and use conservative care such as decompression and adjustments to take pressure off.

Car Accident Injuries

Whiplash, neck and back pain, and headaches after a car crash can show up hours or days later. We examine you, document your injuries and build a recovery plan.

Neuropathy Symptoms

Burning, numbness or pins and needles in the hands or feet can affect daily life. We evaluate your symptoms and coordinate with your medical doctor when needed.
