Chiropractic care

What is chiropractic care?

Chiropractic care is a drug-free, hands-on approach to problems of the spine, joints, muscles and nerves. It has been practiced since 1895 and focuses on restoring healthy movement so your body can function and heal well.

What is a chiropractic adjustment?

An adjustment is a precise, controlled movement, usually by hand, applied to a joint that is stuck or not moving properly. Restoring that movement can ease pain and stiffness, and many patients notice a difference right away.

Does an adjustment hurt?

Usually not. If you already have pain or muscle spasm, moving the area can feel uncomfortable for a moment. Some people feel mild soreness afterward, which usually fades within a day.

Why does a joint make a popping sound during an adjustment?

Not every adjustment makes a sound. When it does, it is gas being released inside the joint as the pressure changes, much like cracking your knuckles. It is harmless.

Is chiropractic care safe?

Chiropractic care is a conservative, non-invasive treatment. Mild soreness after a visit is the most common side effect. Your exam and health history help us choose techniques that are safe and comfortable for you.

How long does a chiropractic appointment take?

Your first two visits take about 45 minutes each for your consultation, exam and first treatment. Follow-up visits usually take 10 to 20 minutes.

Do I have to keep coming back forever?

No. You can stop care once your pain is resolved or your treatment plan is complete. Some patients choose occasional maintenance visits to help prevent problems from coming back, but that is always your choice.

Does everyone get adjusted the same way?

No. Every patient gets a plan built for their condition, age, size and comfort, and we choose techniques to match.

Why do chiropractors take x-rays?

When needed, x-rays show the structure and alignment of your spine and help us spot problems that would change how we treat you, so your care is safe and targeted.

Can I adjust my own back or neck?

We don't recommend it. Twisting your own neck or back usually moves joints that are already loose, not the one that is stuck, and it can make things worse.

Can I see a chiropractor after back surgery?

Yes, in many cases. Chiropractic care can help with stiffness and pain that remain after surgery. We review your surgical history first and adapt our techniques to it.
