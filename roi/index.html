📋 My cheat sheet: what is a new patient worth?

1. Ask them (best)

"What's a typical first visit worth to you?"
"How many times does a new patient usually come back in a year?"

Let them say the number out loud. Then the ROI is theirs, not yours.

2. Quick math

Worth per new patient: $0

3. Look it up

  • Their own website and Google profile: look for a price list, "new patient special", consultation fee or flat fees.
  • Doctors and dentists: FAIR Health Consumer. Free: enter their zip code and a procedure to see typical local costs (medical and dental).
  • Lawyers: Clio lawyer rates by state and practice area. US average is about $362/hr (2026).

4. Rough starting points if they won't say

  • Doctor ~$600 · Dentist ~$900 · Chiropractor ~$700 · Med spa ~$500 per new patient (these load automatically when you pick the practice type)
  • Lawyer: hourly rate × hours per case. Example: $362 × 10 hrs ≈ $3,600 per client
