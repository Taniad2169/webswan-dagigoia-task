Disclosures and authorization

1. Who we are

WebSwan Business Service (operated by WebSwan Digital Solutions) is a private document-preparation and filing-assistance service. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting for the IRS, any Secretary of State or Department of State, any Department of Revenue, or any city, county or other government agency.

2. Not legal, tax or accounting advice

We are not a law firm, attorneys, CPAs or tax advisors, and we do not give legal, tax or financial advice. We cannot tell you which business structure, tax election or licenses are right for you. Using our service does not create an attorney-client relationship. For advice, please consult a licensed attorney or tax professional. Information we share is general and educational.

3. You can do this yourself

You may file your LLC, apply for licenses and apply for an EIN directly with the government without using our service. The IRS issues EINs for free at irs.gov. You are paying WebSwan for preparation help, convenience and support, not for the government documents themselves.

4. Our fee is separate from government fees

The WebSwan service fee is separate from and in addition to all government filing, license, publication, registered agent and other third-party fees. Government fees shown are estimates based on published information at the time of review and may change. We will not pay any government fee on your behalf without your separate approval.

5. No guarantees

Signing this form does not create an LLC, issue an EIN or grant a business license. Only the government agency can approve, issue or reject a filing. We cannot guarantee name availability, approval, processing times or that every license or permit your business needs has been identified. License requirements depend on your city, county, state, industry and activities, and professional or occupational licenses are not included unless agreed in writing.

6. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for the accuracy and completeness of the information you provide and for reviewing documents before they are filed. You remain the owner and responsible party of your business. For EIN applications, the responsible party must be the actual individual who owns or controls the business. Ongoing requirements after filing (for example, annual or biennial reports, franchise tax reports, New York's LLC publication requirement, renewals and taxes) are your responsibility unless you purchase a separate service for them.

7. Sensitive information

Do not send Social Security numbers, tax ID numbers, bank or card numbers, or identity documents through this form or by regular email. When a filing requires them, we will use a secure method or you will enter them yourself directly with the agency. We keep only what is needed to provide the service.

8. Authorization

You authorize WebSwan to prepare the documents for the service you selected and, after your final review and approval, to submit them to the appropriate agency on your behalf, and, where the agency allows it, to act as your third-party designee or preparer for that filing only.

9. Payment and refunds

The WebSwan service fee is paid by secure card checkout (Stripe) right after you sign. Paying the service fee does not pay any government fee. If you cancel before we begin preparing your documents, your WebSwan fee will be refunded. Once preparation has started, the fee is refundable only for work not yet performed. Government fees that have already been paid to an agency cannot be refunded by WebSwan.

10. Technology and AI tools

We may use software, including AI writing tools, to help draft documents and messages. A person at WebSwan reviews the work, and you approve documents before they are filed.

11. Electronic signature and records

You agree to sign and receive these disclosures and records electronically, and that your typed and adopted signature has the same effect as a handwritten signature. You may request a paper copy at no cost, or withdraw your consent to electronic records, by contacting us. Please print or save a copy for your records.
